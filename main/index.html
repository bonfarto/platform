Roles

Who benefits from the platform?

Whether you're a nutritionist, endocrinologist, or obesity specialist, our platform is designed to support your work in treating obesity more effectively.

Dietitians
Create highly personalized meal plans that consider the patient's medical history, lifestyle, and preferences.
Endocrinologists
Manage complex metabolic issues related to obesity, including hormonal imbalances and insulin resistance.
Psychologists
Support patients in addressing the psychological aspects of obesity, such as emotional eating or body image concerns.
Gastroenterologists
Treat obesity-related digestive issues such as gastroesophageal reflux disease or irritable bowel syndrome.
Diabetologists
Manage obesity-related type 2 diabetes and other metabolic disorders.
Exercise Physicians
Develop customized fitness programs that align with the patient's abilities, preferences, and weight loss goals
Obesity Specialists
Oversee comprehensive care and manage co-existing conditions (e.g., hypertension, diabetes) alongside obesity treatment.
Scientists
Conduct research on obesity treatment, uncover new insights, and validate clinical approaches.

Use Cases and Scenarios

See the Platform in Action

Here’s how our platform supports healthcare teams in delivering better outcomes for patients with obesity.

See more use cases
  • Personalized Treatment for Obesity

    A patient with obesity has underlying metabolic issues like insulin resistance or hypothyroidism that complicate weight loss. The endocrinologist uses the platform to get a detailed analysis of the patient’s genetic, metabolic, and lifestyle factors, enabling them to create a personalized treatment plan that addresses the root causes of obesity.


    Learn more
  • Multi-disciplinary Collaboration

    A patient struggles with both emotional eating and metabolic imbalances. The platform helps create a collaborative treatment plan where each specialist (endocrinologist for metabolic issues, dietitian for nutrition, and psychologist for behavioral support) can input their recommendations, ensuring a cohesive, holistic approach to treatment.


    Learn more
  • Personalized Meal Planning & Nutrition Advice

    A patient with obesity needs a highly personalized meal plan to meet specific health goals. The platform analyzes the patient’s dietary preferences, health conditions, and weight loss targets to generate a customized meal plan. The nutritionist then fine-tunes the plan based on AI-generated insights and progress reports.

    Learn more

  • Integrating Genetic Data for Tailored Solutions

    A patient’s obesity has genetic factors at play (e.g., a gene linked to slower metabolism). The platform integrates genetic testing results to provide the endocrinologist with a better understanding of the patient’s metabolic response to different diets and treatments, allowing for more precise interventions.


    Learn more
  • Identifying Behavioral Triggers for Weight Loss Resistance

    A patient with obesity has not responded to conventional weight loss treatments. The platform tracks behavioral and psychological data to identify patterns (e.g., stress eating, emotional eating) that may be hindering progress. The psychologist uses the data to develop specific interventions targeting these behavioral triggers.


    Learn more
  • Chronic Disease Management Alongside Obesity Treatment

    A patient with obesity also has type 2 diabetes and hypertension. The platform provides an integrated view of the patient’s conditions, allowing both the endocrinologist and cardiologist to coordinate treatment strategies, adjust medications, and track health metrics, ensuring a comprehensive approach to obesity management.


    Learn more
  • Early Intervention for Pre-Obesity Patients

    A patient is at risk of developing obesity but has not yet been diagnosed. The platform analyzes the patient’s lifestyle and health data, offering predictive insights that indicate the likelihood of obesity. The primary care physician can then intervene early with preventative strategies, offering guidance on diet, exercise, and behavior changes before obesity fully develops.


    Learn more

Frequenlty asked questions:

  • What is Blago?

    Our AI platform is designed to support specialists in the treatment of obesity by providing data-driven insights, personalized recommendations, and real-time patient monitoring. The platform integrates patient data (e.g., medical history, activity levels, dietary habits) to create tailored treatment plans, track progress, and improve outcomes. It helps dietitians, doctors, and other healthcare professionals deliver more effective and personalized care.

  • Who can benefit from using this platform?

    This platform is specifically designed for healthcare professionals involved in obesity treatment, including dietitians, nutritionists, doctors, and obesity specialists. It’s also a valuable tool for researchers working in obesity-related fields, providing real-world data and insights for evidence-based studies.

  • How secure is the data on this platform?

    Patient privacy and data security are our top priorities. The platform complies with industry standards and regulations, including HIPAA (in the U.S.) and GDPR (in Europe), to ensure that all patient information is securely stored and processed. We implement encryption, secure authentication, and regular security audits to protect user data.

  • Can the platform be used for remote patient monitoring?

    Yes, the platform can be integrated with wearable devices and mobile apps to monitor patients remotely. This allows healthcare providers to track real-time data, such as activity levels, weight changes, and dietary habits, even when patients are not in the clinic.

  • How do I get started with the platform?

    Getting started is simple! You can sign up through our website and request a demo to explore the features. Once you're on board, our team will guide you through the onboarding process, helping you integrate your patient data and set up your account. We also provide training materials to help you make the most out of the platform’s features.
